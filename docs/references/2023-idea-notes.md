# Original Idea Notes (2023)

The first notes for this game, then called "Office Manager". They list the responsibilities of an engineering manager that stood out as learning opportunities. Not every item is meant to become a mechanic; the design draws on them as source material (see the HLD's *Drawn from real management work*). The names of employer-internal tools are replaced with "internal tooling", the names of commercial tools with what they were used for, and employer-specific program names and abbreviations with generic terms.
