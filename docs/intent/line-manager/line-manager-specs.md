# Line Manager Level — Specs

Design: [line-manager-design.md](line-manager-design.md)

## Starting organization

- [x] **LINE-ORG-001**: When a run starts, the line manager level shall generate from the seed one team led by the player with five active engineers of varied skill, motivation, stress, wants and personalities, at least three of the personalities different, and two active projects on that team, one on schedule and one at risk.
- [x] **LINE-ORG-002**: The line manager level shall take the names of generated people and projects from its fixed list of fictional names, and shall not give two people, or two projects, in a run the same name; once every listed name is taken, it shall add to a listed name the lowest number from 2 up that makes it unused ("Settings redesign 2").
- [x] **LINE-ORG-005**: No project name in the line manager level's list of fictional names shall contain a team name from the list, so a project never reads as another team's work.
- [x] **LINE-ORG-003**: When runs are started from the same seed, the line manager level shall generate identical simulation and level states.
- [x] **LINE-ORG-004**: The line manager level shall give every engineer it generates, at the start or on hiring, one personality from the personality catalogue.

## Level parameters

- [x] **LINE-WEEK-001**: The line manager level shall declare the level id `line-manager`, a time step of 1 week, a limit on story situations per week, and a review every 13 weeks.
- [x] **LINE-WEEK-002**: The line manager level shall produce at most its tuned cap of level moments in a week.

## Visibility

- [x] **LINE-VIS-001**: When the player's view is requested, the line manager level shall show each active engineer on the player's team with their name, role, tenure, personality and performance level.
- [x] **LINE-VIS-002**: The line manager level shall show a person's motivation, stress and skill only as bands, never as numbers.
- [x] **LINE-VIS-003**: The line manager level shall show a person's want as unknown until a 1:1 with them has been held, and from then on as their want.
- [x] **LINE-VIS-004**: The line manager level shall not show a person's trust.
- [x] **LINE-VIS-005**: The line manager level shall show each of the team's active projects with its progress, deadline and whether it is on schedule, behind or overdue, and its risk and tech debt only as bands.
- [x] **LINE-VIS-006**: The line manager level shall show the player's energy, the inverse of their stress, and their reputation only as bands.
- [x] **LINE-VIS-009**: The line manager level shall map every banded value to the same scale of bands over 0–100.
- [x] **LINE-VIS-011**: The line manager level shall show, for each engineer, the notes of their history entries that carry one and whose cause is not a habit, newest first, with a note repeated in consecutive entries shown once with how many times ("Took on a design review, twice"), up to three lines.
- [x] **LINE-VIS-012**: The line manager level shall show the current habits, the open vacancies with their interview counts, and the review period's goals with their progress.
- [x] **LINE-VIS-013**: The line manager level shall show each moment's choices without those that name an engineer who is no longer active on the team.
- [x] **LINE-VIS-014**: The line manager level shall show a career summary: the number of the team's projects completed by their deadline over the run, each engineer who joined the team after it started, and each engineer who left it with whether they quit, were let go or moved.
- [x] **LINE-VIS-015**: The line manager level shall show whether the player's stress is at or above the burnout threshold, the number of consecutive weeks they have ended at or above it, and how many such weeks end the run.
- [x] **LINE-VIS-016**: When the player's view is requested, the line manager level shall mark each engineer on the team who is restless.

## Habits

- [x] **LINE-HABIT-001**: When a run starts, the line manager level shall set night pages to a rotation, 1:1 cadence to fortnightly, routine approvals to you, ceremonies to full, growth focus to nobody and the breather to nobody.
- [x] **LINE-HABIT-002**: When the player changes a habit, the line manager level shall apply the new setting to that week's end-of-week habit effects and to the moments of later weeks, leaving moments already produced unchanged.
- [x] **LINE-HABIT-003**: If a habit change names an unknown habit or option, or an engineer who is not active on the team, then the line manager level shall reject it with an error and leave the level state unchanged.
- [x] **LINE-HABIT-004**: When a week begins or its end applies and an engineer a habit names is no longer active on the team, the line manager level shall set routine approvals back to you or growth focus back to nobody, and report the change in that week's recap.
- [x] **LINE-HABIT-005**: When a week's habit effects apply, the line manager level shall submit them to the simulation core as one batch, each effect carrying its habit's id as its cause.
- [x] **LINE-HABIT-010**: When a week's end applies, the line manager level shall resolve each on-call escalation that had no moment that week (because night pages is set to you, it arrived while it was, or there was no engineer to put on call) as taken by the player, raising the player's stress for each.
- [x] **LINE-HABIT-011**: When on-call escalations arrive and night pages is set to a rotation, to seniors or to a backup, the line manager level shall make a moment for the engineer on call of each escalation whose difficulty band is above that engineer's skill band, and under a backup also above the backup's: under a rotation or a backup, the active engineers in id order, one per week in turn, taking every escalation that week; under seniors, the most skilled active engineer, ties broken by id; the backup being the most skilled active engineer other than the one on call, ties broken by id, and none when there is no other.
- [x] **LINE-HABIT-017**: When a week's end applies, the line manager level shall resolve each on-call escalation whose difficulty band the on-call engineer's skill band reaches as handled by that engineer, raising their stress by the same amount as leaving a page to them, adding the history note "Handled a night page alone", or "Handled a night page with a backup" when night pages is set to a backup, applying no consequence, and reporting in the night pages habit's recap line how many pages each engineer handled, on their own or, under a backup, with whom as backup.
- [x] **LINE-HABIT-018**: When a week's end applies with night pages set to a backup, the line manager level shall resolve each on-call escalation whose difficulty band is above the on-call engineer's skill band and that the backup's skill band reaches as handled by the two of them: the on-call engineer's stress rising by the same amount as leaving a page to them, with the history note "Handled a night page with a backup", and the backup's stress rising by the same amount as the player's backing someone up, with the history note "Backed up a night page"; applying no consequence; and reporting in the night pages habit's recap line how many pages each pair handled.
- [x] **LINE-HABIT-019**: Where a line manager situation's conditions count an engineer's night pages by the history note "Handled a night page alone" or "Handled a night page with you", they shall count the notes "Handled a night page with a backup" and "Backed up a night page" in the same way, unless the situation is about who was on call with them: left alone (`pages-alone`) or joined by the player (`pages-together`).
- [x] **LINE-HABIT-020**: While the breather habit names an engineer on the team, the line manager level shall leave them out of the night pages rota and the backup, give them no routine work, and, when a week's habit effects apply, lower their stress by `breatherRecovery`, add a history entry, lower the progress of each of the team's active projects by `breatherProgress`, and say in the recap that they had a breather; when they leave the team, the habit shall fall back to nobody and the recap shall say so.
- [x] **LINE-HABIT-021**: When the line manager level says in the recap that an engineer had a breather, it shall say, in the week the breather began for them, that it takes a few weeks to show; and in a later week of the same engineer's breather unbroken, how many weeks in a row it has run, and their stress band as the week began beside their stress band in the week it began, in the UI's band words: eased from one to the other, still at it, or risen from one to the other.
- [x] **LINE-HABIT-012**: When a week's habit effects apply, the line manager level shall hold a 1:1 with each engineer due one under the 1:1 cadence (every week; every other week, engineers at even positions in id order in even weeks and odd positions in odd weeks; or never), raising their trust and motivation, recording their want as learned, adding a history entry, and raising the player's stress by a small amount per 1:1 held, summed for the week.
- [x] **LINE-HABIT-013**: When routine items arrive and routine approvals is set to you, the line manager level shall resolve each and raise the player's stress by a small amount per item, summed for the week.
- [x] **LINE-HABIT-014**: When routine items arrive and routine approvals names an engineer, the line manager level shall resolve each whose difficulty band that engineer's skill band reaches and that the engineer did not raise themselves, raising their stress a little and serving a want for autonomy; resolve the rest, the engineer's own items and those beyond them, as signed by the player, as when routine approvals is set to you; and report "{name} signed {n} routine approval(s).", followed, when any were beyond them, by "{m} was beyond them, so you signed it." ("{m} were beyond them, so you signed them." for more than one).
- [x] **LINE-HABIT-015**: When a week's habit effects apply with ceremonies set to full, the line manager level shall raise each engineer's motivation a little, serving a want for stability, and raise the player's stress a little; with ceremonies set to light, it shall raise the risk of each of the team's active projects.
- [x] **LINE-HABIT-016**: When a week's habit effects apply with growth focus naming an engineer, the line manager level shall raise that engineer's skill, serving a want for growth, add a history entry, and raise the player's stress.

## Work

- [x] **LINE-INBOX-001**: When work arrives, the line manager level shall add work items whose number and kinds depend on the team's active projects, their risk and tech debt, and its engineers, drawing all randomness from the level's own seeded generator and leaving the simulation's generator state unchanged.
- [x] **LINE-INBOX-002**: When work arrives while the team has fewer than two active projects, an engineer on it is strained or more stressed, no work intake item is open, and no work intake was declined or expired in the eight weeks before, the line manager level shall add a work intake item.
- [x] **LINE-INBOX-003**: The line manager level shall give each work item a unique id, a kind, a subject, a size, a difficulty, and a last week equal to its arrival week plus its kind's expiry weeks.
- [x] **LINE-WORK-001**: The line manager level shall hand release, access and expense approvals to the routine approvals habit, on-call escalations to the night pages habit, routine design reviews and customer requests to the team (LINE-WORK-013, LINE-WORK-015), and make every other design review, customer request and work intake a work moment.
- [x] **LINE-WORK-013**: When the line manager level makes the week's moments, it shall hand each open design review on a project whose tech debt band is at most the pay-down band, and whose difficulty band the skill band of an engineer whose stress band is below strained reaches, to the least stressed such engineer (ties broken by id), and make no moment for it.
- [x] **LINE-WORK-017**: When the line manager level makes the week's moments and two or more open design reviews on projects whose tech debt band is at most the pay-down band are left without an engineer only because every engineer whose skill band reaches them is strained or above, it shall make one work moment for all of them, counting as one toward its cap, offering to do them all yourself, to give each to the least stressed engineer whose skill band reaches it, and to push them all back; and a choice in it shall apply to each review, in turn, as the same choice in that review's own moment would.
- [x] **LINE-WORK-015**: When the line manager level makes the week's moments, it shall hand each open customer request whose difficulty band is below the skill band of an engineer whose stress band is below strained to the least stressed such engineer (ties broken by id), and make no moment for it.
- [x] **LINE-WORK-014**: When a week's end applies, the line manager level shall resolve each design review or customer request it handed to an engineer still on the team as taken by them, raising their stress by the delegation stress, serving wants for growth and autonomy, adding the history note "Took on a design review" or "Took on a customer request", applying no consequence, and reporting "{name} took {n} design review(s)." and "{name} took {n} customer request(s)." in its recap's habits part; an item whose engineer has left stays open.
- [x] **LINE-WORK-002**: When the line manager level makes a work moment, it shall offer doing it yourself, giving it to the least stressed engineer whose skill band reaches the item's difficulty band, giving it to the least stressed engineer whose skill band falls short of it (ties broken by id), the trade its kind allows, and pushing back, leaving out the item's requester and any suggestion with no engineer to fill it, and for work intake offer taking it on and declining instead.
- [x] **LINE-INBOX-004**: When the player does a work item themselves, the line manager level shall resolve it without its consequence and raise the player's stress by an amount that grows with its size.
- [x] **LINE-INBOX-005**: When the player gives a work item to an engineer, the line manager level shall raise that engineer's stress, tagged as serving wants for growth and autonomy, add a history entry, and resolve the item.
- [x] **LINE-INBOX-006**: When a work item given to an engineer has a difficulty above their skill, the line manager level shall apply the reduced version of its consequence and raise their skill.
- [x] **LINE-INBOX-007**: When several work items are given to one engineer in a week, the line manager level shall raise that engineer's stress once for each.
- [x] **LINE-WORK-003**: When the player pushes back on a work item, the line manager level shall leave it open to expire at its last week.
- [x] **LINE-INBOX-009**: When a week's end applies, the line manager level shall remove each open work item whose last week is the current week, and apply its consequence with a level rule as the cause.
- [x] **LINE-INBOX-010**: When a work item's subject is no longer valid (a project no longer active, a requester no longer active on the team), the line manager level shall remove it without its consequence.
- [x] **LINE-INBOX-011**: When the player takes on work intake, the line manager level shall start a new project on the team through the simulation core, named from the level's list, raise the player's stress by its size, and raise each engineer's stress by a tuned amount, noting that they took on new work while stretched.
- [x] **LINE-INBOX-012**: When the player declines work intake, the line manager level shall lower the player's reputation by less than the work intake's expiry consequence.
- [x] **LINE-INBOX-015**: When the line manager level adds a work intake item, it shall give it one of three kinds by its item number in turn: a launch, a handover, or a rewrite for the engineer on the team who wants growth or autonomy and whose want has gone unserved longest (ties broken by id), and a launch in place of the rewrite when there is no such engineer.
- [x] **LINE-INBOX-016**: When the player takes on work intake, the line manager level shall, beyond LINE-INBOX-011, raise the player's reputation by the launch reputation for a launch, start the project at the handover tech debt for a handover, and for a rewrite raise its engineer's motivation by the rewrite motivation, serving growth and autonomy, with the note "Got the rewrite they wanted".
- [x] **LINE-INBOX-017**: When the player declines work intake, the line manager level shall lower the player's reputation by the launch decline reputation for a launch, the handover decline reputation for a handover, and its decline reputation for a rewrite, and for a rewrite lower its engineer's motivation by the rewrite decline motivation, with the note "Saw the rewrite they wanted turned down".
- [x] **LINE-INBOX-018**: When the line manager level describes a work intake moment, its title and text shall name the offer's kind and what it asks of the team, and for a rewrite name its engineer.
- [x] **LINE-INBOX-014**: When a week's end applies and the team ends it with fewer than two active projects, no engineer on it strained or more stressed, no work intake item open, and no work intake declined or expired in the eight weeks before, the line manager level shall start a new project on the team through the simulation core, named from the level's list, and its recap for that week shall name the project as one the player's manager assigned.
- [x] **LINE-INBOX-013**: When a week's end applies and the team has ended that week and the three weeks before with no active project, the line manager level shall start a new project on the team through the simulation core, named from the level's list, and its recap for that week shall name the project as one the player's manager assigned.
- [x] **LINE-WORK-005**: When the line manager level makes an on-call moment, it shall offer rolling the change back (see LINE-WORK-012), backing up the engineer on call (a little stress for the player; the engineer's stress rises, and a difficulty above their skill brings the reduced consequence and raises their skill), taking over (the player's stress rises by the escalation's size, with no consequence), and leaving it to them (no cost to the player; the engineer's stress rises, and a difficulty above their skill brings the full consequence).
- [x] **LINE-WORK-004**: When a level moment's choice applies, the line manager level shall return a reaction from its templates for that kind of moment and choice, chosen by the personality of the engineer involved where there is one.
- [x] **LINE-WORK-016**: When the player does a work item themselves, the line manager level's reaction shall say that the work came out of the player's own energy.
- [x] **LINE-WORK-012**: When the player rolls back the change behind a night page, the line manager level shall lower the project's progress by `rollBack.progress` (never below zero), raise the engineer on call's stress by `rollBack.stress`, apply none of the escalation's consequence, raise no one's skill, and resolve it.
- [x] **LINE-WORK-006**: When a level moment's choice involving an engineer applies, and the engineer has a history entry from the last `callbackWeeks` weeks whose note the level's callback table holds, that no reaction has called back, and that is not a night page's note when the moment is a night page, the line manager level shall end the reaction with the table's line for the most recent such entry, filled with the engineer's name, and record that entry as called back.
- [x] **LINE-WORK-007**: The line manager level shall call back each history entry at most once in a run, and never one whose note its callback table does not hold.
- [x] **LINE-WORK-008**: When the line manager level makes a design review work moment for a project whose tech debt band is at least `payDown.band`, it shall offer fixing the debt the review touches first.
- [x] **LINE-WORK-009**: When the player fixes the debt a design review touches, the line manager level shall lower the project's progress by `payDown.progress` and its tech debt by `payDown.techDebt`, raise the player's stress by `payDown.stress`, and resolve the item.
- [x] **LINE-WORK-010**: When the line manager level makes a customer request work moment, it shall offer a smaller change.
- [x] **LINE-WORK-011**: When the player offers a smaller change for a customer request, the line manager level shall lower the player's reputation by `smallerChange.reputation`, less than the request's expiry consequence, raise nobody's stress, and resolve the item.

## Moments

- [x] **LINE-MOMENT-001**: When a week begins, the line manager level shall produce its moments for the week, up to its cap, in this order: a final evaluation moment per engineer held for one; an on-call moment per escalation the night pages habit gives an engineer; a check-in moment per project due one; an interview moment per open vacancy; and work moments for open items, including those pushed back or left unanswered in earlier weeks, closest to expiring first.
- [x] **LINE-MOMENT-002**: The line manager level shall give each moment a day of the week and a time: final evaluation moments on Monday afternoon, on-call moments at night, check-in moments on Monday morning, interview moments on Wednesday, and work moments through the week in the order they were made.
- [x] **LINE-MOMENT-003**: When the player picks a choice for one of the week's open level moments, the line manager level shall apply it at once and close the moment; if the moment is not open or does not offer the choice, it shall return an error and leave the states unchanged.
- [x] **LINE-MOMENT-004**: When a week's end applies, the line manager level shall close each level moment left unanswered as letting it wait: a work item stays open, an interview is skipped, a check-in changes nothing, and an on-call escalation expires with its consequence.
- [x] **LINE-MOMENT-005**: When the line manager level describes an on-call moment, its text shall say that a change from the escalation's project has taken production down and name the engineer on call.
- [x] **LINE-MOMENT-006**: When the line manager level describes a design review, its text shall be one of the kind's phrasings picked by the item's number, name the project and its tech debt band, and say that the debt rises if nobody reviews the design before it expires.
- [x] **LINE-MOMENT-007**: When the line manager level describes a customer request, its text shall be one of the kind's phrasings picked by the item's number, name the project and a customer from the level's list of fictional companies picked by the item's number, and say that the player's reputation slips if nobody answers before it expires.
- [x] **LINE-MOMENT-008**: The line manager's situation content shall include, apart from onboarding, at least five situations of normal priority, marked `quietWeek`, whose conditions can all hold together for a team in which no engineer is strained or above, the team's stress is below strained, and no project is overdue, behind schedule or at high risk, and cannot hold for the same team once every engineer is strained and a project is behind schedule.
- [x] **LINE-MOMENT-009**: Each line manager situation marked `quietWeek` shall be marked `once`.
- [x] **LINE-MOMENT-010**: Where a line manager quiet-week situation follows another, its id being that situation's id followed by `-later`, it shall bind as its person only someone who remembers being in the first situation, and only from 13 weeks after the first was presented, and each of the six calm-team situations that otherwise came back most (`calm-loan`, `calm-prototype`, `calm-slack`, `calm-stretch`, `calm-ceremony`, `calm-pairing`) shall have one.
- [x] **LINE-MOMENT-011**: The line manager situations `scope-creep`, `conference-talk` and `review-friction` shall each be marked `once` and have a follow-up, its id being that situation's id followed by `-later`, that binds as its people only the people who were in the first situation, and only from 13 weeks after the first was presented, whichever choice was made.
- [x] **LINE-MOMENT-012**: The `scope-creep` situation shall name an engineer on its project's team as the one who would build the request, and each of its choices shall leave that engineer a history entry.
- [x] **LINE-MOMENT-013**: The line manager situations `flight-risk-1on1` and `improvement-plan` shall bind as their person only someone who does not remember being in them, and each shall have a follow-up, its id being that situation's id followed by `-later`, whose person role holds only for someone who remembers being in the first, from 13 weeks after it, whichever choice was made, and otherwise on the first's own conditions, and for someone who does not remember being in the follow-up.
- [x] **LINE-MOMENT-014**: The line manager situation `remote-out-of-loop` shall not be presented once the player has resolved it, earlier in the run, by moving the team's decisions into writing.

## Project check-ins

- [x] **LINE-CHECK-001**: When a week begins, the line manager level shall make a project due a check-in if it is one of the team's active projects, is behind schedule, overdue, or has a risk band at or above its tuned check-in band, and has had no check-in moment in the tuned number of weeks before.
- [x] **LINE-CHECK-002**: When the line manager level makes a check-in moment, it shall record the week as the project's last check-in and offer re-planning it, cutting scope, moving the deadline, and pushing on.
- [x] **LINE-CHECK-003**: When the player re-plans a project at a check-in, the line manager level shall raise the player's stress and lower the project's risk by their tuned amounts.
- [x] **LINE-CHECK-004**: When the player cuts a project's scope at a check-in, the line manager level shall reduce its scope by the tuned share of its remaining work, by at least 1, and lower the player's reputation by its tuned amount.
- [x] **LINE-CHECK-005**: When the player moves a project's deadline at a check-in, the line manager level shall move it out by the tuned number of weeks and lower the player's reputation by more than cutting scope does.
- [x] **LINE-CHECK-006**: When the player pushes on at a check-in, the line manager level shall change nothing.
- [x] **LINE-CHECK-007**: When the line manager level describes a check-in moment, its text shall name the first of these that holds of the project as the reason: overdue, behind schedule, or a risk band at or above the check-in band.

## Hiring

- [x] **LINE-HIRE-001**: The line manager level shall set the team's target headcount to its number of engineers when the run starts.
- [x] **LINE-HIRE-002**: When the simulation has advanced, and when a week begins to commit, if the team's active engineers plus its open vacancies are below its target headcount, the line manager level shall open vacancies until they reach it.
- [x] **LINE-HIRE-003**: When a vacancy reaches the required number of interviews, the line manager level shall hire a generated engineer with a personality onto the team through the simulation core and close the vacancy.
- [x] **LINE-HIRE-004**: When the player answers an interview moment, the line manager level shall add an interview to the vacancy, raising the player's stress if they interview and the chosen engineer's stress (with a little recognition) if an engineer does, or add none if the player skips.

## Goals

- [x] **LINE-GOAL-001**: When a review period begins, the line manager level shall set two or three goals for it from its fixed set, according to the team's state and its own generator, offering a ship-by-deadline goal only for an at-risk project whose deadline falls within the period, and recording that deadline in the goal.
- [x] **LINE-GOAL-002**: When the player's view is requested, the line manager level shall show each goal's progress toward its target, with any week number in it shown counting from 1 (game week *w* as week *w* + 1), and the on-schedule goal's progress as "No projects running" when the team has no active projects.
- [x] **LINE-GOAL-003**: When the level is assessed for a review, the line manager level shall add each of the period's goals as a measure that is above target when achieved and below target when missed (a ship-by-deadline goal achieved only if the project completed by the deadline the goal recorded), leaving out a goal about an engineer who is no longer on the team and saying so in the recap.
- [x] **LINE-GOAL-004**: The line manager level shall count the on-schedule goal as achieved when every active project is on schedule, or, with no active project, only when the team ended no more than half the review period's weeks idle.
- [x] **LINE-GOAL-005**: The line manager level shall count the goal to bring the team's stress down as achieved when the team's average stress is below the goal's line, and show its progress in the stress band's words: "Done: the team is {band}" when achieved, and otherwise "Not yet: the team is {band}, and this needs it {the band below the line}".

## Recap

- [x] **LINE-RECAP-001**: When a week's end applies, the line manager level shall report what each habit did, how each level moment was answered and what it led to, which work items expired, and the period's goal progress.
- [x] **LINE-RECAP-002**: When a week's end applies, the line manager level shall report each engineer whose motivation, stress or skill band changed that week (a skill band that rose as grown in skill, and a stress band that rose with up to two of the level's own causes of that week's stress rises for them, largest first, in words), or who gained a history note that week whose cause is neither a habit nor the end-of-period evaluation, with each distinct note recorded for them that week once, leaving out the evaluation's note, which the recap's evaluations list carries, and how many times it was recorded when more than once.
- [x] **LINE-RECAP-006**: When a week's end applies, the line manager level shall report at most one recalled memory: among the team's engineers, the most recent history entry from before that week and from the last `callbackWeeks` weeks whose note the callback table holds and that no reaction or recap has called back (ties broken by engineer id), as the table's line filled with that engineer's name, and record that entry as called back.
- [x] **LINE-RECAP-004**: When asked after an advance who quit in the week just played, the line manager level shall report each engineer of its team who quit that week, with the weeks their motivation had been below the low line and their stress above the high line, whether their trust band was guarded or below, and the note of their most recent remembered history entry, if any.
- [x] **LINE-RECAP-005**: When asked after an advance how the team's projects moved in the week just played, the line manager level shall report each of the team's projects still active after the advance whose progress any cause changed, with its progress at the start of the week and after the advance, its scope, and the amount, when not zero, that each cause added or took away: each kind of the level's choices that changed progress, approvals that expired or fell short, the week's other changes before the advance (the situations), the simulation's setback rule and its project work rule.
- [x] **LINE-RECAP-003**: When asked after an advance which projects shipped in the week just played, the line manager level shall report each of the team's projects completed in that week, with how many weeks after its deadline it was completed, or none when it was completed by its deadline.
- [x] **LINE-RECAP-007**: When asked after an advance for the week's watch line, the line manager level shall report at most one, the first of these that holds and that LINE-RECAP-009 does not hold back: the player's stress at or above `burnoutThreshold`, with the weeks in a row it has been there and the weeks left before burnout; a restless engineer of its team, the one with the fewest weeks left before their notice runs out (ties broken by engineer id); an engineer of its team whose motivation is below the simulation's low motivation line or whose stress is above its high stress line, the one with the most weeks over either line (ties broken by engineer id); an engineer of its team whose stress rose this week into the band that holds the high stress line without crossing it, or whose motivation fell this week into the band that holds the low motivation line without crossing it, or whose stress or motivation was in that band without crossing its line at the start of each of the last `watchWeeks` weeks and still is (ties broken by engineer id); an active project of its team that is overdue (overdue first), behind schedule, or whose deadline is within `watchWeeks` weeks and whose remaining scope is more than its progress over the last `watchWeeks` weeks scaled to the weeks left (soonest deadline first, ties broken by project id); and none when none holds or the player burned out that week.
- [x] **LINE-RECAP-008**: When the line manager level reports a watch line about an engineer, it shall say in words who it is and which line they are over or nearing (the one they have been over longer when over both, stress on a tie), with the weeks over it when over, and name one cause: for stress, the largest of the level's own causes of that engineer's stress rises over the last `watchWeeks` weeks, in the recap's words, when there is one, and when it is a habit's (night pages, routine approvals, or coaching under the growth focus), that habit by name and in how many of the last `watchWeeks` weeks it added to their stress; for motivation, that what they want has gone unserved for the weeks since it was last served, when the want the player learned is still their want and it has gone unserved for longer than the simulation's want drift threshold, or that the player does not know what they want, when the player has not learned their current want.
- [x] **LINE-RECAP-009**: The line manager level shall not report a watch line of the same kind about the same subject (the player, an engineer or a project) as one it reported within the last 3 weeks, unless the earlier line was of a kind later in LINE-RECAP-007's order, or the line is about the player's stress and one week is left before burnout.
- [x] **LINE-RECAP-010**: When the line manager level reports a watch line about a restless engineer, it shall say "{name} is restless and may leave within {n} week(s)", where {n} counts this week as the first of those left before their notice runs out, followed by ", unless they get {what they want}." when the player has learned their current want, in LINE-RECAP-008's words for wants, or ", and you don't know what they want." when not.
- [x] **LINE-RECAP-011**: When the watch line warns about an engineer's stress, the line manager level shall end it with "A breather would take them off night pages and routine work for a while.", unless the breather habit already names them.

## Reviews and the end of the level

- [x] **LINE-END-001**: When the scorecard is requested, the line manager level shall report the number of the team's projects completed by their deadline in the 13 weeks before the scorecard's week, and, when the team has active projects, the share of them neither overdue nor behind schedule, or no share when it has none.
- [x] **LINE-END-002**: When the scorecard is requested, the line manager level shall report the number of engineers who quit the team in the 13 weeks before the scorecard's week, and how many of them were rated meets or above.
- [x] **LINE-END-003**: When the scorecard is requested, the line manager level shall report the team's average motivation and average stress, and the share of its active engineers who have had a 1:1 in the four weeks before the scorecard's week.
- [x] **LINE-END-004**: When the level is assessed for a review, the line manager level shall report whether promotion is possible, which it is from week 39 (the third review) on, how many reviews remain until it is, and how many reviews it has left after this one, its last being the eighth.
- [x] **LINE-END-005**: When the simulation has advanced, if the player's stress has been at or above the burnout threshold for four consecutive weeks, then the line manager level shall report that the player has burned out.
- [x] **LINE-END-006**: When the simulation has advanced, the line manager level shall record the week in which each of the team's projects became completed.
- [x] **LINE-END-007**: When the level is assessed for a review, the line manager level shall report as measures the projects completed on time and the on-schedule share (higher is better), the quits rated meets or above (lower is better), the average motivation (higher is better), the average stress (lower is better) and the 1:1 share (higher is better), each with the target and tolerance its tuning sets, leaving out the on-schedule share when the team has no active projects, unless the team ended more than half the review period's weeks with no active project, when the share counts as 0.
- [x] **LINE-END-008**: When the level is assessed for a review, the line manager level shall report that it is the level's last review at week 104 (the eighth review), and not before.
- [x] **LINE-END-009**: When the level is assessed for a review while the team's average stress is in the overwhelmed band, and the team was overwhelmed for no more than half the review period's weeks, the line manager level shall set a rating ceiling of `meets` with the reason "Your manager won't call it a strong quarter with the team this stretched."
- [x] **LINE-END-010**: When a week's end applies, the line manager level shall count the week as overwhelmed if the team's average stress after it is in the overwhelmed band, counting from zero again in the first week of each review period.
- [x] **LINE-END-011**: When the level is assessed for a review after the team was overwhelmed for more than half the review period's weeks (7 of 13), the line manager level shall set a rating ceiling of `partial` with the reason "Your team spent most of the quarter overwhelmed."
- [x] **LINE-END-012**: When a week's end applies, the line manager level shall count the week as idle if the team has no active project after it, counting from zero again in the first week of each review period.
- [x] **LINE-END-013**: When a week's end applies and none of the player's choices in the level's moments that week raised their stress, the line manager level shall lower the player's stress by its tuned rest recovery.
- [x] **LINE-EVAL-001**: When the last week of a review period ends, the line manager level shall rate each active engineer who has been on the team since the period began and has no rating recorded in the period other than the starting organization's at their corresponding rating, unless that rating would be their third low rating in a row, with the note "Rated <rating> on what they delivered", the rating in words (below expectations, partially meets, meets, exceeds, outstanding).
- [x] **LINE-EVAL-002**: When the line manager level rates engineers at the end of a review period, its recap for that week shall name each engineer it rated and the rating.
- [x] **LINE-EVAL-003**: When the line manager level does not rate an engineer at the end of a review period because the rating would be their third low rating in a row, it shall hold a final evaluation moment for them, on Monday afternoon of the next week, offering to rate them on what they delivered, which lets them go through the simulation core, and to give them one more quarter.
- [x] **LINE-EVAL-004**: When the player gives an engineer one more quarter, or leaves their final evaluation moment unanswered at the week's end, the line manager level shall record no rating for them for the period just ended and add the note "Given one more quarter".
- [x] **LINE-EVAL-005**: When the line manager level holds a final evaluation, its recap for the last week of the period shall name the engineer as waiting for the player's decision.
- [x] **LINE-EVAL-006**: Where a line manager situation offers to rate an engineer on what they delivered, and that rating would be a low one while the engineer is on an improvement plan, the choice offered shall be labelled as letting them go ("…, and let them go"), and the unlabelled rating shall not be offered.
- [x] **LINE-EVAL-007**: Where a line manager situation rates an engineer and records a note, the note shall name the rating given ("Rated partially meets on what they delivered").
- [x] **LINE-EVAL-008**: Where a line manager calibration situation (`calibration`, `calibration-no-slot`) settles that an engineer does not get the top rating, the choice shall rate them at once at the rating its text announces (*meets* for the one who loses the top rating, *partially meets* for the one traded down), with a note naming the rating; where it settles that an engineer keeps the top rating, it shall record no rating for them, leaving the end of the period to rate them on what they delivered.
- [x] **LINE-EVAL-009**: Where a line manager calibration situation offers to argue, the reputation at or above which arguing holds shall be the lower edge of a reputation band, and the situation's text shall name that band and the band above it, if any, in the bands' words ("a good or excellent reputation").
- [x] **LINE-EVAL-010**: Where the player argued in a line manager calibration situation (`both` or `both-again` in `calibration`, `argue` or `argue-again` in `calibration-no-slot`) within the last 20 weeks, the situation shall offer its argument as a choice whose label says the player argued at the last calibration (`both-again`, `argue-again`) in place of the usual one, holding only with a reputation one band higher than otherwise, and not at all when no higher band is left, and costing twice the reputation the usual one does.
