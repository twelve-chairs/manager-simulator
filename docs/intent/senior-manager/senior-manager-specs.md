# Senior Manager Level — Specs

Design: [senior-manager-design.md](senior-manager-design.md)

The bands a value is shown in are the line manager level's five bands of a 0–100 value; a band shown *better* is the healthier one (higher motivation, lower stress and attrition risk), and a band already at its best stays there. A month is four weeks, and months and reviews count from the start of this level. *Energy* is the player's stress seen the other way up, as at the line manager level: costing energy raises the player's stress. Regretted attrition is heads lost whose rating was *meets* or better. A value or rate the specs call *tuned* is set in the level's tuning, by the balance harness and playtesting.

## Handover

- [x] **SENIOR-HAND-001**: When a line manager review promotes the player, the senior manager level shall offer a handover choice of who leads the player's team: each active engineer on it, shown with their skill, trust in the player and want, or an outside hire.
- [x] **SENIOR-HAND-002**: When the player chooses an engineer to lead their old team, the senior manager level shall make that engineer the team's manager, with management skill in the *developing* band if their trust in the player is at least 60 and their want is growth, and in the *learning* band otherwise, and treat the team as having lost a head.
- [x] **SENIOR-HAND-003**: When the player chooses an outside hire to lead their old team, the senior manager level shall add a new manager to it with management skill in the *capable* band, trust of 50 and a want drawn from the level's generator, and raise the team's stress by a tuned amount.
- [x] **SENIOR-HAND-004**: When the handover is applied, the senior manager level shall convert the player's old team to aggregate form under its new manager, keeping its projects, and add two teams to the player's group, each with a generated manager, headcount, health and one or two projects, every project named unlike any other project in the state.
- [x] **SENIOR-HAND-005**: When the handover is applied, the senior manager level shall keep the player's energy, reputation and rating history, and leave the old team's engineers in the state as aggregated people with their histories.
- [x] **SENIOR-HAND-006**: If a handover choice names neither an active engineer on the player's old team nor an outside hire, then the senior manager level shall reject it with an error and leave the state unchanged.

## Organization

- [x] **SENIOR-ORG-001**: The senior manager level shall give each generated manager a name, personality, want, management skill, motivation, stress and trust drawn from the level's generator, and a leniency from 0 to 2 rating steps.
- [x] **SENIOR-ORG-002**: The senior manager level shall give each team in the player's group at most one manager, and shall treat a team whose manager has left as leaderless until a new manager is set.

## Visibility

- [x] **SENIOR-VIS-001**: When the player's view is requested, the senior manager level shall show each manager in the group with their name, personality, team, performance level, memory, motivation, stress and management skill as bands, and their want once a 1:1 with them has been held.
- [x] **SENIOR-VIS-002**: When the player's view is requested, the senior manager level shall show each team under its manager with its headcount, motivation and stress as bands, attrition risk as a band, and each of its projects with progress against scope, deadline and schedule status.
- [x] **SENIOR-VIS-003**: While a team's manager has management skill below the *capable* band or trust in the player below 40, the senior manager level shall show that team's motivation, stress and attrition risk one band better than their true bands, unless the team's truth is shown.
- [x] **SENIOR-VIS-004**: While the player's oversight of a team's manager is close, or in a month in which a skip-level session listened to that team, the senior manager level shall show that team's true bands and say that they come from what the player saw.
- [x] **SENIOR-VIS-007**: When the player's view is requested, the senior manager level shall also give the habits, the month's moments with their kind, title, text, choices and the managers their choices depend on (and, for calibration, each team's entry), the player's energy and reputation as bands with whether they are at the edge and their months there, and the career so far: the months played, the managers who joined the group and those let go.
- [x] **SENIOR-VIS-008**: When the player's view is requested, the senior manager level shall mark each manager who is restless.
- [x] **SENIOR-VIS-005**: The senior manager level shall never show a manager's leniency as a value.
- [x] **SENIOR-VIS-006**: The senior manager level shall show no individual engineer of a team in aggregate form, except as named in situations and the recap.

## Habits

- [x] **SENIOR-HABIT-001**: When the level begins, the senior manager level shall set oversight to coaching for every manager, 1:1s with managers to fortnightly, backfill to hold the bar, skip-level listening to none and new work to decide each time.
- [x] **SENIOR-HABIT-002**: When the player changes a habit, the senior manager level shall apply the new setting from that month's end-of-month effects and to the moments of later months.
- [x] **SENIOR-HABIT-003**: If a habit change names an unknown habit or option, or a manager who is not in the group, then the senior manager level shall reject it with an error and leave the level state unchanged.
- [x] **SENIOR-HABIT-004**: When a month ends, the senior manager level shall cost the player a tuned amount of energy for each manager under coaching oversight and a larger tuned amount for each under close oversight, and raise the management skill of each manager under coaching oversight by a tuned amount.
- [x] **SENIOR-HABIT-005**: When a month ends with a manager under close oversight, the senior manager level shall lower that manager's motivation by a tuned amount if their want is autonomy, and lower their trust by a tuned amount once they have been under close oversight for three months in a row.
- [x] **SENIOR-HABIT-006**: When a month ends, the senior manager level shall hold 1:1s with each manager at the habit's cadence, each raising that manager's trust and revealing their want, at a tuned energy cost per 1:1.
- [x] **SENIOR-HABIT-007**: When a team loses a head, the senior manager level shall backfill it after one month at a tuned lower skill under fast backfill, and after two months at the team's skill under hold the bar, raising the team's stress by a tuned amount for each month the head is missing.
- [x] **SENIOR-HABIT-008**: When a month ends under monthly skip-level listening, the senior manager level shall listen to the next team in rotation, show that team's truth for the month, surface one of its problems its manager has not raised as a moment the next month that names it (a project of the team's behind schedule; else stress strained or worse; else attrition risk moderate or worse; else motivation low or worse; else one of ten everyday frictions, four of which are beyond what the team's manager can settle alone and are named so in the moment's text: one raised for that team before and left unsettled, if any, else the first in turn, from one varying by month and team, that the player has not settled for that team in the last `issueSettledWeeks` weeks, and no moment when every one has been), cost the player a tuned amount of energy, and lower the team's manager's trust by a tuned amount if it is below 50.
- [x] **SENIOR-HABIT-011**: When the player answers a problem skip-level listening surfaced, the senior manager level shall record it for that team as settled when the answer fixed it (stepping in; for a problem its manager can settle, coaching a manager whose management skill is at least developing, or leaving it with one at least capable) and as unsettled otherwise; and when it surfaces for that team a problem recorded as unsettled, the moment shall say that the engineers raised it again and how the player answered it last time.
- [x] **SENIOR-HABIT-012**: When the player coaches a manager through, or leaves with them, a surfaced friction beyond what that manager can settle alone, the senior manager level shall not ease the team's stress, and its reaction shall say the problem is not the manager's to fix; coaching shall still cost its energy and grow the manager's skill.

## Teams under their managers

- [x] **SENIOR-DRIFT-001**: When a month ends, the senior manager level shall move each team's motivation and stress a tuned share of the way toward the levels its manager's management skill sustains, and further when the player's oversight of that manager is hands-off and their skill is below the *capable* band.
- [x] **SENIOR-DRIFT-002**: When a month ends, the senior manager level shall set each team's attrition risk from its motivation and stress, as tuned.
- [x] **SENIOR-DRIFT-003**: When a month ends, the senior manager level shall move each manager's motivation by whether their want was served that month and their stress toward their team's stress, as tuned.

## Moments

- [x] **SENIOR-MOMENT-001**: When a month begins, the senior manager level shall produce its moments for the month, up to a tuned cap, in this order: final evaluations of managers held for one; setting a new manager for a team that has none; calibration, in the last month before a review; problems surfaced by skip-level listening; escalations; staffing requests; cross-team disputes; approvals; new work for the group; and restructuring.
- [x] **SENIOR-MOMENT-002**: The senior manager level shall give each moment a week of the month, a day and a time, in order through the month.
- [x] **SENIOR-MOMENT-003**: When a team's stress or attrition risk is in the *high* band or worse, or one of its projects is behind, the senior manager level shall be able to produce an escalation from its manager, offering to coach them through it, step in and fix it yourself, or let them handle it.
- [x] **SENIOR-MOMENT-004**: When the player steps in on an escalation, the senior manager level shall fix the problem by a tuned amount, cost the player a tuned amount of energy and leave the manager's management skill unchanged; when the player coaches, it shall fix it by a smaller tuned amount if the manager's skill is at least *developing*, raise their skill and cost less energy; when the player lets them handle it, it shall fix it only if their skill is at least *capable*.
- [x] **SENIOR-MOMENT-005**: When one team's stress is in the *strained* band or worse while another's is *steady* or better, the senior manager level shall be able to produce a staffing request, offering to move a head between them, backfill with a hire, or decline; moving a head shall lower the receiving team's stress and lower the giving manager's trust if their team's stress then reaches *busy* or worse.
- [x] **SENIOR-MOMENT-006**: When two teams have active projects at risk at once, the senior manager level shall be able to produce a cross-team dispute between their managers, offering to get them to agree a plan, decide it yourself, or let it run; agreeing shall raise both managers' management skill and lower both projects' risk by a tuned amount, with a reaction that says the shared work is planned; deciding shall lower the winning project's risk by a larger tuned amount and the trust of the manager whose project gives way; and letting it run shall raise both projects' risk.
- [x] **SENIOR-MOMENT-014**: When the player agrees a plan for or decides a cross-team dispute, the senior manager level shall record the two teams as settled, and shall not produce a dispute between those two teams until a tuned number of weeks (a quarter) has passed, producing it instead between the first pair, taking teams in order of risk, that is not settled; letting a dispute run shall record nothing.
- [x] **SENIOR-MOMENT-007**: When a team has no active project, and the new-work habit is to decide each time or the team's stress band is strained or above, the senior manager level shall be able to produce new work for the group, offering to give it to the idle team, named, to give it to them and set the new-work habit to give work to teams with room from then on, to decline it, or to spread it across all teams; spreading it shall raise every team's stress.
- [x] **SENIOR-MOMENT-013**: When a month ends under the new-work habit of giving work to teams with room, with a team that has no active project and a stress band below strained, and no new work was a moment that month, the senior manager level shall start a new project on that team through the simulation core, named from the level's list, and its recap shall name the project and the team as work the player's manager assigned.
- [x] **SENIOR-MOMENT-015**: When the senior manager level produces new work for the group, it shall make it one of three kinds by the month of the level in turn, a launch, a handover or the idle team's own project, name the kind in its title and text, say in the labels of giving and declining it what each gains or costs for that kind, and react to the choice by what it did.
- [x] **SENIOR-MOMENT-016**: When the player takes new work for the group (giving it, giving it from then on, or spreading it), the senior manager level shall also raise the player's reputation by the launch reputation for a launch, start the project at the handover tech debt for a handover, and raise the idle team's motivation by the own-project motivation for its own project; and when the player declines it, shall lower the player's reputation by the launch decline for a launch, the handover decline for a handover, and its usual decline for the team's own project, also lowering that team's motivation by the own-project decline motivation.
- [x] **SENIOR-MOMENT-017**: While the new-work habit is to decide each time, the senior manager level shall produce new work for the group for a team whose stress band is below strained at most once every `newWorkOfferWeeks` weeks.
- [x] **SENIOR-MOMENT-018**: When a month begins and a manager leading one of the group's teams is restless, the senior manager level shall produce a stay conversation about them, after final evaluations, teams needing a manager and calibration and before every other moment, whose text says what they want from the job, record that want as known, and offer to give them it, in a label naming that want, or to wish them well.
- [x] **SENIOR-MOMENT-019**: When the player gives a restless manager what they want in a stay conversation, the senior manager level shall raise their motivation by the stay motivation, tagged as serving their want, and their trust by the stay trust, and raise the player's stress by the stay energy; wishing them well, or letting the conversation wait, shall change nothing; and once the manager has left, neither answer shall change anything.
- [x] **SENIOR-MOMENT-008**: When a manager proposes a promotion or letting someone go in their team, the senior manager level shall be able to produce an approval, offering to approve, ask for the evidence first, or refuse. Approving shall move one of the team's heads up a rating (a promotion) or remove one head (letting go), and lower the team's motivation by a tuned amount if the manager's leniency is above 0; asking for the evidence shall apply it only if the manager's leniency is 0, and lower the manager's trust by a small tuned amount either way; refusing shall lower the manager's trust by a tuned amount.
- [x] **SENIOR-MOMENT-009**: When the group has run 12 months with three teams, the senior manager level shall be able to produce a restructuring that adds a fourth team, offering to promote its manager from one of the teams, which lowers that team's skill, or hire from outside, which leaves the new team leaderless for a month.
- [x] **SENIOR-MOMENT-010**: When the player picks a choice for one of the month's open level moments, the senior manager level shall apply it at once and close the moment; if the moment is not open or does not offer the choice, it shall return an error and leave the state unchanged.
- [x] **SENIOR-MOMENT-011**: When a month's end applies, the senior manager level shall close each level moment left unanswered as letting it wait.
- [x] **SENIOR-MOMENT-012**: The situation content tagged for the senior manager level shall contain no effect that lowers an aggregate team's headcount.

## Calibration

- [x] **SENIOR-CAL-001**: When the calibration moment is presented, the senior manager level shall show for each team its manager's proposed ratings, which are the ratings its delivery corresponds to raised by the manager's leniency, beside that delivery, and the group's curve: the most top ratings (*exceeds* and *outstanding*) the group may hold, `curveShare` of its heads rounded down.
- [x] **SENIOR-CAL-010**: The senior manager level shall derive a team's delivered ratings from a score of its skill, motivation and stress: the score falls between two ratings by the tuned delivery thresholds, and, of its heads less `deliverySpread` of them a rating below and as many a rating above, the share in proportion to how far the score has climbed from the lower threshold toward the higher shall be at the higher rating and the rest at the lower, so that a one-point rise in the score never moves more than a few heads.
- [x] **SENIOR-CAL-002**: When the player calibrates a team, the senior manager level shall accept the proposal or move heads up or down a rating as the player chooses, apply the result with re-rate effects, and react through the manager: pleased at an accepted proposal, and losing trust by a tuned amount for each head moved below their proposal.
- [x] **SENIOR-CAL-003**: If a calibration moves more heads than a team holds at a rating, or names a rating not on the scale, then the senior manager level shall reject it with an error and leave the state unchanged.
- [x] **SENIOR-CAL-005**: When a month ends with the calibration moment unanswered, the senior manager level shall apply each manager's proposal as proposed, and then, if the group holds more top ratings than its curve, have the room trim them.
- [x] **SENIOR-CAL-004**: When calibration applies, the senior manager level shall change each team's motivation by how far its ratings fall short of or exceed what it delivered, as tuned.
- [x] **SENIOR-CAL-006**: When every team in the calibration moment is settled and the group holds more top ratings than its curve, the senior manager level shall keep the moment open and offer to argue for an exception or to let the room trim.
- [x] **SENIOR-CAL-007**: When the player argues for an exception to the curve, the senior manager level shall lower the player's reputation by `curveExceptionCost`, twice that when the player argued at the group's calibration within the last `curveMemoryWeeks` weeks, let the settled ratings stand if the player's reputation was at least `curveExceptionReputation`, the lower edge of the good reputation band, or `curveExceptionAgainReputation`, the lower edge of the excellent band, when the player argued at the group's calibration within the last `curveMemoryWeeks` weeks, and otherwise have the room trim them.
- [x] **SENIOR-CAL-009**: When calibration is settled over the curve and the player argued at the group's calibration within the last `curveMemoryWeeks` weeks, the choice to argue shall say that the player argued at the last calibration and that the room will hold an exception only for an excellent reputation.
- [x] **SENIOR-CAL-008**: When the room trims, the senior manager level shall move top-rated heads to *meets* one at a time, an *exceeds* before an *outstanding*, until the group holds no more top ratings than its curve, each from the team whose ratings then stand furthest above what it delivered, then from the team with the most top ratings, ties broken by team id; each trimmed head shall cost its manager trust as a head the player moves below their proposal does, each trimmed team's motivation shall change as SENIOR-CAL-004 sets for its new ratings, and the reaction shall name each team that lost a top rating and how many.

## Managers' plans

- [x] **SENIOR-PLAN-001**: When a review period ends, the senior manager level shall rate each manager on their team's delivery and health under them in that period.
- [x] **SENIOR-PLAN-002**: When a manager has been rated below *meets* at two period ends in a row, the senior manager level shall put them on an improvement plan, and when a third low rating would be due, hold it for a final evaluation moment, offering to let them go or give them one more period.
- [x] **SENIOR-PLAN-003**: When the player lets a manager go, the senior manager level shall remove them and leave their team leaderless; and when a month begins with a team in the group that has no manager and none on the way, it shall offer a moment to set a new manager: promote from the team, lowering its skill, or hire from outside, leaving it leaderless one more month.

## Goals

- [x] **SENIOR-GOAL-001**: When a review period begins, the senior manager level shall set two or three goals for it from the group's state, drawn from: grow a named manager's management skill by a band, keep regretted attrition at zero, ship a named project by its deadline as it stood when the goal was set, and bring a named team out of the *strained* band.
- [x] **SENIOR-GOAL-002**: When the player's view is requested, the senior manager level shall show each goal with its progress.
- [x] **SENIOR-GOAL-003**: When the level is assessed for a review, the senior manager level shall add each of the period's goals as a measure, scored as achieved or missed.

## Recap

- [x] **SENIOR-RECAP-001**: When a month ends, the senior manager level shall return a recap of what changed (each manager whose band changed or who gained a memory note other than from a habit, each team whose reported health changed band, heads who left or joined, and what slipped) and of the routine (what each habit did, and each goal's progress).
- [x] **SENIOR-RECAP-003**: When asked after an advance who quit in the month just played, the senior manager level shall report each of its managers who quit that month, with the weeks their motivation had been below the low line and their stress above the high line, whether their trust band was guarded or below, and the note of their most recent history entry with a note, if any.
- [x] **SENIOR-RECAP-004**: When asked after an advance which of the player's teams lost heads in the month just played, the senior manager level shall report each team whose headcount fell while the simulation advanced, with its name, how many heads it lost, and how many of them were rated meets or better.
- [x] **SENIOR-RECAP-002**: When asked after an advance which projects shipped in the month just played, the senior manager level shall report each project of the player's teams completed in that month, with its team and how many weeks after its deadline it was completed, or none when it was completed by its deadline.

## Reviews and the end of the level

- [x] **SENIOR-END-001**: The senior manager level shall advance the simulation four weeks per turn.
- [x] **SENIOR-END-002**: The senior manager level shall be reviewed every six months, at months 6, 12, 18, 24, 30 and 36 of the level.
- [x] **SENIOR-END-003**: When the scorecard is requested, the senior manager level shall report delivery (projects across the group completed by their deadline in the period, and the share of active projects on schedule), retention and team health (regretted attrition, and the teams' average motivation and stress), managers' growth (the change in the managers' average management skill over the period, and whether each team's health held), and calibration quality (one less the rating steps by which the period's ratings strayed from what each team delivered, per head of the group, leaving out steps down no more than the room's trim of the delivered ratings to the curve would take).
- [x] **SENIOR-END-004**: When the level is assessed for a review, the senior manager level shall report whether promotion is possible, which it is from the third review (month 18) on, how many reviews remain until it is, and how many reviews it has left after this one, its last being at month 36.
- [x] **SENIOR-END-005**: When the level is assessed for a review, the senior manager level shall report that it is the level's last review at the sixth review (month 36), and not before.
- [x] **SENIOR-END-006**: When the simulation has advanced, if the player's stress has been at or above the line manager level's burnout threshold at the end of four consecutive months, then the senior manager level shall report that the player has burned out.

## Turn loop contract

- [x] **SENIOR-CONTRACT-001**: The senior manager level shall provide the turn loop's career level contract with a time step of four weeks, its moments' days counted from the month's first Monday (week of the month × 5 + day), handover choices from the line manager state, a take over operation that takes the line manager state, the simulation and the handover choice and returns the simulation and this level's state, or an error, and the calibration moment answered as a whole: `acceptAll`, or `settle:` followed by a JSON object giving each team's moves, with any team it leaves out settled as proposed.

## Level state

- [x] **SENIOR-STATE-001**: The senior manager level shall keep its state (habits, the managers' leniency, the month's moments, goals, visibility for the month, oversight history, plans, settled disputes and the burnout and review counters) serializable as plain data, derived only from the run's seed and commands.
